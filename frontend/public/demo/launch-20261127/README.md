# PKR launch screenshots

These images show the actual PKR application with fictional River City Poker records. They contain no production account, player, payment or invitation data.

The thirteen WebP images are used by the public homepage and product guides. Two original PNG captures are included for the social preview renderer. `manifest.json` lists their routes, viewport sizes and source capture. Phone screenshots show the web app viewport; they do not simulate a device status bar.

The complete gallery is produced by `.github/workflows/pkr-launch-gallery.yml` using `frontend/scripts/capture-launch-gallery.cjs`. Browser requests are isolated to synthetic fixtures and the local build. The downloadable gallery includes the original PNGs and overview boards.
